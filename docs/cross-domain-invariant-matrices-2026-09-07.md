### Existing critical E2E: what its source actually establishes

[critical-offline-lifecycle-exam.spec.ts](../e2e/critical-offline-lifecycle-exam.spec.ts#L28) contains three relevant journeys:

1. Existing student attendance offline → reconnect → persisted attendance. Does not create parent entity then remap a dependent Grade or inject local ACK failure.
2. Semester lock through UI rejects grade writes and premature promotion. Does not change explicit academic-year date range or current policy after finalization.
3. Smart Exam server-authoritative MC scoring → Grade. Does not append a manual daily attempt after that finalize, archive a year after single approval, or round-trip protected lifecycle through backup.

Chưa chạy lại browser E2E trong audit này. PASS lịch sử của ba case không chứng minh các missing combinations trên. Top 10 invariant regressions và roadmap theo root cause nằm ở §7–8 của [finding report](cross-domain-integration-invariant-audit-2026-09-07.md).
